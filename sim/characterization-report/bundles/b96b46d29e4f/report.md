## Measurement notes

- I_off is a gmin-floor-limited upper bound (gmin = 1e-13 S), set jointly by true device leakage and the numerical floor; it is not a resolved subthreshold current.
- Capacitor leakage comes from the stock cap model, which has no DC branch; it is a model-structural value, not a measurement of oxide leakage.
- R_on is the real part of the small-signal impedance at 10 MHz; it carries a single-digit-percent to ~10% capacitor-geometry sensitivity, so it is reported per sub-configuration.
- At the sf process corner Candidate 3's fixed gate overdrive is not fully on (R_on in the MOhm range); those values are reported as measured, not filtered.
- Sizing and geometry levels are the netlist defaults and a x2 width, not the ratified split-axis levels.
- Values are pre-breakdown, stock-model results at rated-voltage validity; nothing here predicts silicon behavior from assumed model parameters.
