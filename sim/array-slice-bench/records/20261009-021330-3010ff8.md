## Reading the results (added by hand when the record was created; the tables above are generated)

* **Reach: met everywhere.** In every region and every one of the four capacitor x access sub-combinations, over all 5 process x 3 temperature x 3 supply corners and every selectable cell, the selected cell conducts with eta >= 0.88 (lowest: Region III min/min, ss/125 C/-10 %), the return current matches the bus current, and the residual plate voltage is < 0.03 % of the bit-line level. Region II (the 10 V asymmetric stage) is the strongest (eta >= 0.984).
* **Isolation: met except one cell of the matrix.** Every unselected-cell class (`same_col`, `same_row`, `other`) and every idle bus is below the 1e-3 threshold in all regions **except the idle Region I bus at ff / 125 C**, which exceeds it at all three supply levels (24 of the 360 Region I `bus` instances; worst ratio 1.31e-3 = 4.07 nA against an ideal of 2.7 uA). That current is the OFF-state leakage of the two `pg_n06` column pass-gates attached to the bus in this slice (placeholder `wpg` = 20 u), not of the cells (the worst unselected *cell* in Region I is 6.5e-4, `same_row`, also ff/125 C). It is reported as measured; the threshold was fixed before the run and has not been relaxed. In the full array a band has 10-11 such pass-gates on its bus, so this is the `(N-1) x I_off` aggregate of `spec/architecture.md` §3 item 2 showing up early: the real number for the sense reference is ~5x this slice's. Pass-gate sizing is a placeholder here, so the finding is about the structure's OFF leakage at the hot/fast corner, not a verdict on a sized design.
* **Candidate 2 orientation**: confirmed structurally (table above) and by the check in `design/check-array.py`.
* **Not shown by this bench**: the pass-gate threshold drop with `VDDH` equal to the bit-line level (BLG is held 2 V below VDDH), anything dynamic, and anything about breakdown.
