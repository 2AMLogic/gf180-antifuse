# Gate-oxide breakdown model: V_bd versus pulse width, and its spread

Issue #205 (parent #204). A physics note for the thin 3.3 V oxide (Candidates 1
and 3) and the thick 6 V oxide (Candidate 2). Nothing here is measured and
nothing was programmed. Every table between `<!-- gen:... -->` markers is
generated by [`scripts/breakdown_oxide_model.py`](../scripts/breakdown_oxide_model.py)
and checked against this file by `tests/test_breakdown_oxide_model.py`;
regenerate with `python3 scripts/breakdown_oxide_model.py --update`.
`design/breakdown/breakdown_params.spice` is **not** edited by this issue.

Source labels follow `docs/breakdown-vbd-sources.md`: **S1** BVox table, **S2** Cox
table, **S3** design-manual voltage limits. Candidate results are never pooled.

## 1. Model

Field in the oxide: `E = (V - V_off) / tox`. Everything is a function of this
field and the pulse width; polarity is not modelled (the sign convention of the
S1 NCH rows is unstated).

**Median lifetime, E-model** (field-driven bond breaking), for a reference
device of area A0:

    T50(E) = TREF * exp(-gamma * (E - E0))

so `E0` is the median breakdown field of an A0 device at `TREF = 1 us`, and
`gamma` the field acceleration. In the thermochemical reading, gamma = p/kT with
p an effective dipole moment; that reading is not used to pick a value.

**Spread and area (weakest link, Weibull).** A device of area A fails by time t
with probability `F = 1 - exp(-(A/A0) * ln2 * (t/T50)^beta)`, so the time at
quantile q is

    t_q = T50 * ( (-ln(1-q)/ln2) * (A0/A) )^(1/beta)

Inverting at fixed pulse width t gives the quantile field

    E_q(t, A) = E0 + [ ln(TREF/t) + (1/beta) * ln( (-ln(1-q)/ln2) * (A0/A) ) ] / gamma

and `V_bd = V_off + tox * E_q`. Consequences used below: V_bd falls by
`tox/gamma_dec` per decade of pulse width (gamma_dec = decades per MV/cm);
the quantile-to-quantile width is `tox/(beta*gamma) * ln(L2/L1)` with
`L = -ln(1-q)`, independent of t; per decade of area the median moves by
`tox*ln10/(beta*gamma)`.

**Ramp.** Under a field ramp `k = R/tox` the Miner sum `integral dt/T50` gives
the same result as a pulse of width `t_eff = 1/(gamma*k) = tox/(gamma*R)`. S1
states no ramp, so the comparison in section 5 solves for the `t_eff` (and
equivalent ramp R) at which the model reproduces each S1 row.

**Two shape parameters.** `beta_i = 0.4/nm * tox` (percolation-type thickness
scaling, intrinsic) and `beta_x = 1.5` (the existing `bd_beta`, defect-limited
bound). Distribution family: Weibull on time-to-breakdown (extreme-value on
ln t), which follows from the weakest-link assumption; the shape values are
`assumed`, not sourced.

**Regime of validity.** The E-model is a high-field law. At low field the
lifetime is often described by a 1/E law (section 6 brackets the difference),
so extrapolating to DC is the least reliable end. The model has no temperature,
no polarity, no flat-band/depletion dynamics (`V_off` is a constant), no
trapping/charge build-up, no soft-breakdown stage, no edge/perimeter term, and
no process skew of tox beyond the Cox-derived range of section 3. The area
term assumes uniform intrinsic breakdown.

Variant B puts the floor margin comfortably above the S1 floor (the model

`E0` by `ln(100)/gamma`, about 0.7 MV/cm (E0 sensitivity: section 6).

- **P1. Median V_bd at 1 us, 1 um^2, per candidate** lies between the variant A
  value and the variant B value (columns "P1 band"). *Falsified* if a candidate's
  median, from at least 30 structures, is outside its band; that rejects the
  field-driven picture with these `E0`, `V_off` ranges, not just one parameter.
- **P2. Slope with pulse width.** V_bd falls by `tox/gamma_dec` per decade of
  pulse width: 0.4 to 0.2 V/decade (thin, gamma 2 to 4) and 0.76 to 0.38 V/decade
  (thick) over the assumed gamma range; the thick/thin slope ratio equals the
  tox ratio, about 1.9. *Falsified* if a candidate's slope falls outside its
  range over at least three decades of pulse width, or if the thick/thin ratio
  is below 1.4 (a field-independent mechanism).
- **P3. Area scaling.** The median falls by about 0.08 V per decade of area
  (intrinsic) up to 0.18 V (thin) / 0.34 V (thick) per decade (extrinsic bound).
  *Falsified* if the median moves by more than 0.35 V per decade of area for a
  thin candidate (or does not move over two decades), which would mean edge or
  defect dominated breakdown, outside this model.
- **P4. Shape.** Fitted Weibull shape is larger for the thick oxide by roughly
  the tox ratio if intrinsic (3.2 / 6.1 / 3.2). *Falsified* if thick and thin
  shapes agree within the 25% uncertainty that 30 structures give.
- **P5. Spread.** The 10 to 90% V_bd width at fixed pulse width is no wider
  than the extrinsic-bound column and, if intrinsic, about 0.11 V for every
  candidate. *Falsified* by a width above the extrinsic-bound column (including
  by process tox skew, which the model excludes; that outcome would say the
  spread is process-, not oxide-, limited).
- **P6. S1.** If S1 is measured as a ramp V_bd on the same structure, variant A
  predicts it to be below S1 typ at any lab ramp rate (table in section 5.1);
  variant B predicts agreement with S1 typ at `R_S1`. A measured ramp V_bd
  between the two discriminates them.
