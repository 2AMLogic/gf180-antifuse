# Candidate 3 well-junction avalanche model

Issue #208 (parent #204). **Status: evidence note, not a decision record.** It
changes no ratified spec and chooses no lead candidate (operator decision on
#127). Every number is conditional on the parameters tagged `assumed` below;
nothing here is measured on gf180 silicon, and no junction element exists in
any bench it draws on. All tables in sections 3 to 5 are generated by
`scripts/breakdown_c3_junction_model.py` and checked against it by
`tests/test_breakdown_c3_junction_model.py`.

## 1. Structure and which junctions bound the scheme

From `design/netlists/cell_candidate3.spice` (the committed cell, which the
breakdown wrapper `design/breakdown/cell_candidates_bd.spice` instantiates
unchanged):

- `XM1 CN WL BL VB pfet_06v0`: a 6 V PMOS access device. Its bulk is the
  n-well tie `VB`. Drain = `CN`, source = `BL`.
- `XC1 CN PL cap_pmos_03v3`: the thin-oxide PMOS capacitor between `CN` and
  `PL`; the antifuse oxide.

The junctions that can avalanche, with the bias-pair names of
`sim/program-bias-scheme/README.md`:

| # | Junction | Pair | Why it can bound the scheme |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1 | p+ drain (`CN`) / n-well (`VB`) | `cj` | `CN` follows the program voltage, so this junction carries the oxide voltage plus the well offset. Dominant. |
| J2 | p+ source (`BL`) / n-well | `bj` | `BL` is at the return level or at VPP minus the ballast drop; 0 to 3.6 V in every record. |
| J3 | n-well / p-substrate | none (derived) | The substrate is at 0 V, so this junction sees the n-well level: 11.2 V in the BL-driven bench (the well rides VPP), 3.3 V in `plb`, 0 V in `gwa` / `gwb`. |

In every arrangement J1, J2 and J3 are **reverse**-biased: PL-driven benches
pulse PL negative (corner ids `pl-8p80v`) so `CN` goes below the well;
the BL-driven bench takes the well to 3.3 V + VPP while `CN` returns toward
PL = 0 V. Polarity is read from the bench definitions
(`sim/program-bias-scheme/run_experiment.py`, record headers), not from a
recorded signed value.

**PDK anchor (read from the pinned PDK model file `sm141064.ngspice`; the two diode cards
are identical in variants A to D, the `pfet_06v0` card was read in variant B;
2026-10-29).** The model cards carry a breakdown
parameter for each junction: `diode_pd2nw_06v0` (the "6.0V P+/Nwell diode",
file header) `bv = 10.5`; `pfet_06v0` `bvd = bvs = 10.5`;
`diode_nw2ps_06v0` `bv = 14`. These are SPICE model parameters, not
published absolute-maximum ratings: the open PDK documentation gives none
(`spec/candidate3-read-bias-decision.md`, PDK revision paragraph). The
extraction criterion (the model's `ibv` defaults to 1 mA), the tolerance and
the temperature dependence are not stated.

**Labelled unverified** (not confirmed from PDK documentation):

1. That `cap_pmos_03v3` sits in the same n-well as `XM1`. The model is a
   two-terminal C(V) with no bulk pin and no junction. If the capacitor's
   well is a separate net, it adds a junction J1' of the same type whose
   voltage is set by that net, which this note cannot state.
2. That the access device's drain and source are plain p+/n-well junctions
   with the planar profile of the `diode_pd2nw_06v0` card (an extended or
   lightly doped drain would raise `cj`'s avalanche voltage; edge and gate-edge
   effects would lower it).
3. That the 6 V p+/n-well diode card describes the same implants as
   `pfet_06v0`. The equal `bv = 10.5` supports it; it does not prove it.
4. That no deep n-well or other isolation is present in the Region III layout.
   (J3 would then be n-well / deep n-well, a different junction.)

## 2. Parameters and provenance

| Parameter | Value / range | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| p+/n-well `bv` | 10.5 V | pdk (model card) |
| n-well/p-substrate `bv` | 14 V | pdk (model card) |
| tolerance on model-card `bv` | +-20 % | assumed |
| avalanche temperature coefficient alpha | +0.05 to +0.15 %/K (positive) | assumed |
| `bv` read as the value at | 27 degC | assumed (the card has no temperature term) |
| n-well doping at the p+ drain | 5e16 to 2e17 cm^-3 | assumed |
| p-substrate doping at the n-well | 1e15 to 1e16 cm^-3 | assumed |
| curvature / edge derating of the planar value | 0.6 to 1.0 | assumed |
| Si band gap | 1.12 eV | textbook |
| junction model | one-sided abrupt, V_B = 60 (Eg/1.1)^1.5 (N/1e16)^-0.75 V | Sze, *Physics of Semiconductor Devices* |

A graded junction was considered and not used: at equal peak doping it
avalanches at a higher voltage than the abrupt one, so the abrupt value is the
conservative bound. TCAD is not available (no process deck), and a generic
breakdown figure without the doping dependence is the thing the issue rejects.

No doping profile for gf180 is published in the sources reachable here, so
every doping value is `assumed`. The PDK `bv` is the only sourced anchor, and
the doping route is used to say what it implies and how sensitive the result
is.

## 5. Verdict per arrangement

avalanche range, **NOT IN-RATING** above the high bound, and **UNDETERMINED**
between them. "Margin" is bound minus stress; negative means the stress is
above that value. The last column is the number of access-device terminal
pairs over the 6 V class rating in the same records at 1x (#90, #129, #130);
those counts are not computed here and are why a junction verdict is not the
whole answer: a device-rating failure is independent of the junction.

Reading it, per the stated uncertainty:

- **`gwa` and `gwb` (n-well tied at 0 V): the junctions are in rating** at the
  assumed and floor oxide V_bd, with 0.2 to 2.0 V of margin to the *low*
  bound and 3.2 to 5.0 V to the nominal. At the sourced typical V_bd 8.8 V
  `gwa` is **undetermined** (nominal margin +2.4 V, low-bound margin -0.6 V);
  `gwb` stays in rating (+0.13 V to the low bound, a margin inside the
  assumed tolerance). J3 is at 0 V.
- **`plb` and `bl` (n-well at 3.3 V, or riding VPP): undetermined, and the
  nominal margin is negative** at the assumed and typical V_bd (-0.3 to
  -1.5 V against 10.5 V), positive only at the floor V_bd (+0.3, +0.5 V).
  Whether J1 avalanches before the oxide breaks is not decided by the sourced
  datum: the +-20 % tolerance is assumed. Where it does avalanche, the clamp
  (see section 7) stops `CN` rising and the oxide may never reach V_bd.
  `bl` also leaves J3 undetermined (11.2 V stress against a 10.1 V low bound).
- **Device rating, independent of the above:** every arrangement has 1 to 3
  access pairs over the 6 V class at 1x in the committed records
  (`gwa` 2, `gwb` 3 at assumed and typ and 1 at the floor, `plb` and `bl` 3).
  So no arrangement is in rating for *every* device from these records, and
  the junction result does not change that.

**No program bias that is in rating for every junction and every device is
shown to exist from this evidence.** A bias exists whose junctions are in
rating on the sourced datum (`gwa`, `gwb` at assumed and floor V_bd); it does
not meet the access-device rating, which was already the open item in
`spec/program-bias-scheme-proposed-decision.md`.

## 6. What is not bounded, and the datum that would decide it

The verdict for `bl` and `plb`, and for `gwa` at the typical oxide V_bd, is
**not bounded by the sourced data**. The datum that decides it is one of:

1. **The extraction criterion and tolerance of `bv = 10.5` V** for
   `diode_pd2nw_06v0` / `pfet_06v0` (current at which it is defined, lot
   spread, temperature), from the foundry; or
2. **A measured p+/n-well reverse I-V** on the Run-3 structure in section 8,
   at -40 and 125 degC: the voltage at which the reverse current reaches the
   program ballast current (about 20 uA in the benches, 500 uA design
   placeholder).

Neither is available in this tree. This is stated here and not closed with a
guess; the operator is asked to supply or commission (1) or (2).

## 7. Limits of this evidence

- **The benches have no junction.** `cap_pmos_03v3` is a two-terminal C(V) and
  `pfet_06v0`'s diode parameters are not exercised past `bv` in these
  transients. The `cj` numbers are the voltage the bench imposes, valid as a
  reverse-bias stress while the junction is reverse; they are not what a real
  well would settle at. A real J1 that reaches avalanche clamps `CN`, diverts
  the program current from the oxide, and the oxide may not reach V_bd. That
  coupling is **not simulated**; a program-yield claim for `bl` / `plb` should
  wait for it.
- Stress values depend on the assumed oxide V_bd, the placeholder 500 uA
  ballast and the placeholder 100 fF PL shunt of the benches.
- Hot-carrier / snapback of the access device and junction leakage before
  avalanche (the unselected-cell risk named in the program-bias decision) are
  not modelled.
- Curvature (0.6 to 1.0), the tolerance and the doping are `assumed`; the
  verdict uses only the tolerance and temperature range, not the curvature.

## 8. Run-3 prediction and falsification

Structure: a stand-alone p+/n-well diode with the cell's drain geometry (same
implants as `pfet_06v0`'s drain, same well), plus an n-well/p-substrate diode,
each on pads, measured at -40, 27 and 125 degC, with the junction at the cell's
drawn W/L and area/perimeter ladder if shuttle area allows. Any run needs its
own operator go-ahead (`docs/breakdown-vbd-sources.md` section 5).

**Prediction** (from this model; reverse current criterion 1 uA/um of drawn
perimeter, then the same at the ballast current):

1. p+/n-well breakdown at 27 degC lies between 7.6 and 14.5 V, centred near
   10.5 V (the card).
2. It **rises** with temperature, by 0.05 to 0.15 %/K (about +4 % to +15 %
   from 27 to 125 degC, and the same fall at -40 degC).
3. n-well/p-substrate breakdown at 27 degC lies between 10.1 and 19.3 V.
4. Consequence: `gwa` / `gwb` programming shows no junction current at the
   oxide V_bd. `bl` / `plb` shows a junction-current plateau near the p+/n-well
   breakdown.

**Falsified if** any of: (a) the measured p+/n-well breakdown at 27 degC is
below 7.6 V or above 14.5 V, so the model-card anchor is wrong by more than
the assumed tolerance; (b) it **falls** with temperature, so tunnelling
(not avalanche) dominates, which means the junction is much more heavily doped
than the abrupt model assumes and the whole table is invalid; (c) the
measured breakdown at -40 degC is below the `gwa` stress at the measured
oxide V_bd (6.4 to 8.1 V in the table): then the in-rating verdict for the
n-well-at-0-V arrangements is wrong; (d) a junction current plateau appears
in `gwa` / `gwb` programming at oxide-breakdown voltage.
